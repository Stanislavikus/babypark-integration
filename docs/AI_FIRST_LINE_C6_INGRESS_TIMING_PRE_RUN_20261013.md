## Statistical pre-registration and scope
- For bounded low-contention component groups A and A-duplicate, use ten warmup operations followed by **59 individual measured operations**. Why 59: under a hypothetical independent stationary population with a true p95 threshold, 1-0.95^59 = 95.15% chance to see at least one sample from that tail. This is a tail-encounter argument only, NOT 95% confidence in a hard limit, tail quantile bound, or evidence of real independent source traffic. Report raw times, min, median, p95 nearest-rank, max, mean, host load and anomalies; retain synthetic stdout evidence. No computed hard upper bound.
- For lock contentions, use 10 warmups + 59 measured acquisitions per low-hold scenario (20 ms child hold for BEGIN IMMEDIATE and DELETE-mode COMMIT; this short fixture hold is for seeing lock behavior only). Child processes release locks independently of parent event-loop blocking. Each trial obtains both connection-specific PRAGMA busy_timeout before blocking. Use reader/writer in same synthetic DB and no await/yield inside any transaction. Separate 3 purely diagnostic long-wait runs with non-ingress busy_timeout 5000 ms and synthetic hold 60 ms; three runs cannot support any tail inference. Each run has a 180-second outer timeout. Do not intentionally wait 5 seconds on every run.
- For timeout/fail-closed: 10 trials with ingress candidate busy_timeout=15ms and competitor lock hold=60ms, expected SQLITE_BUSY/non-2xx semantic classification, never ACK. Note these are synthetic scenario parameters NOT proposed configured production timeouts. Number 10 is a bounded negative functional repeat, not a statistical performance sample.
- No synthetic load exceeding resource limits or unbounded flood. Reason for limited trial counts is event-loop/log/CPU interference with catalog workload. Always report censored/missing/truncated results as INCOMPLETE; do not change runs, warmups or thresholds after seeing data without new plan revision.
- Confidence treatment: no p99 or hard bound inferred from n=59. The B_wait[j] conservative calculation must charge full owning B_site[j] plus separately evidenced overshoot unless source-enforced completion proof exists. K cannot be inferred from N. In absence of target-equivalent peaks, margin M and finite total work, CODE-STAGE FIT remains HALT regardless of measurements.
